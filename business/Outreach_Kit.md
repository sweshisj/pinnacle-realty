## 6. Where to find the first three clients

- Google Maps: search "real estate agency" in Coburg, Preston, Footscray,
  Dandenong, Werribee — look for independents with dated or missing websites.
- realestate.com.au → Find Agents → filter your suburbs → check each
  agency's own site (many just link their portal profile — perfect prospects).
- Serviced apartments: search "serviced apartments Melbourne" — operators
  with Facebook-page-only presences are ideal (the booking system is your killer feature).
- Property developers advertising land releases / townhouses on roadside
  boards — the number on the board is the decision-maker.
- Your dad's network: ask for two introductions, not "anyone" — specific asks travel further.
